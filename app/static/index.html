      How to use BiKing ·

      Welcome to BiKing 🚲

      BiKing plans bike rides across Kraków that are easier on your body: less heat, cleaner air and less strong sun. For every ride you get two routes:

      • Fastest: the quickest way (dashed).
      • More comfortable: usually a few minutes longer, with more shade and cleaner air (solid).

      Set your route

      • Search a place: type a street, an address or a place (Wawel, Rynek Główny, Rusznikarska 17) and pick it from the list. The first one is the start A, the next the destination B.
      • Or click the map. Add up to 3 stops on the way the same way.
      • Drag a marker to move it; ✕ in the Waypoints list removes it.
      • 📍 Start from my location uses your phone's or laptop's position as A.
      • Just looking? ▶ Load demo route shows a ready example.

      Say when and who is riding

      • Scenario: Live now uses today's weather and air quality; Heatwave and Smog are recorded days to try the extremes.
      • Depart: when you set off. Shade, heat and sun change during the day.
      • Profile: Asthma / Allergy puts clean air first, Senior / Child feels the heat sooner, Athlete rides faster and breathes harder.
      • Routes update by themselves. Advanced options let you choose what to avoid: heat, air pollution or UV.

      Read the map

      • The selected route is coloured by discomfort: comfortableuncomfortable Point at a stretch to see why: heat, air or UV.
      • Click a route or its card to switch between the two.
      • Show discomfort map colours every street around you the same way (zoom in to street level).

      Compare and choose

      • More comfortable vs fastest shows what the extra minutes buy: fine dust breathed in, shade, felt temperature, heat stress and strong sun. Green is better, red is worse; point at a name for an explanation.
      • How much extra time is comfort worth? Move the slider to trade minutes for comfort. It appears when there is a real choice.
      • Health tips and 🔊 Read aloud sum up the ride.

      Take it with you

      • ⬇ GPX on a route card saves the route for Komoot, Garmin Connect, Strava and other apps.
      • ♿ Accessibility: colours for colour-blind users, bigger text, high contrast and less motion. 🌙 switches to dark mode.
      • You can open this guide again with ? at the top.
